# Porsche Taycan EV Sales Trend Analysis (2021–2024)

## Project Question
How has the introduction of the Taycan affected Porsche's market 
position in the luxury EV segment?

## Tools Used
- Python (pandas, Plotly, SQLite)
- SQL (window functions: LAG, RANK, PARTITION BY)
- Microsoft Excel (formulas, KPI dashboard)
- Google Colab

## Files
- `Porsche_Taycan_Analysis.ipynb` — full analysis notebook (run in Google Colab)
- `porsche_taycan_queries.sql` — all 7 SQL queries with comments
- `porsche_taycan_analysis.xlsx` — Excel workbook (Annual Data, Quarterly Sales, KPI Summary)

## Key Findings
- Taycan peaked at 41,296 deliveries in 2021, outselling the iconic 911
- Taycan held the #1 spot in luxury EV segment every year vs Tesla Model S and Lucid Air
- Revenue grew 7.7% in 2023 despite delivery fluctuations — showing pricing power
- 2024 saw a structural 39% delivery decline, not seasonal — driven by rising competition
- Weak correlation (~0.3) between Taycan volume and total revenue shows Porsche's 
  broader product mix matters

## Background
This project is an extension of an IB Business Management research paper 
analyzing Porsche AG's strategic shift toward electric vehicles.
